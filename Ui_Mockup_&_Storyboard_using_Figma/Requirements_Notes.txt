Entrants/events
"Allows user to browse and search for events, using both plaintext and tags"


Entrants/events/all events
"Offers info on events like waitlist size, dates, geolocation requirements, and allow entering and canceling waiting list"


entrants/my-events
"Allows users to see the events they signed up for and their current statuses like waiting, selected, rejected, or to confirm"


entrants/notifications
"Allows users to see their notifications"


entrants/profile
"Allows users to see their profiles and all their data like they're phone/phone number, email, and name"


Organizer/events
"Allows organizers to create and browse their events"


Organizer/Create
"Allows an organizer to fully detail the event they want to create, the dates, capacity, pricing, poster, geolocation, tags, and create QR code"


Organizer/manage/allEvents
"Allows organizers to manage their events including exporting a CSV for waitlist and confirmed list"


/admin
"dashboard for admin view"


/admin/events
"allows admins to manage events and see flagged ones and to determine whether to remove them or not"


/admin/profiles
"Allows admins to manage and view users and remove harmful users"


/admin/images
"allows admins to browse through potentially harmful event images and choose whether or not to remove them"
